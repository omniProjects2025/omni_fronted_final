Why should I go for a health checkup package?

Regular health checkups help in early detection of medical conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, thyroid issues, heart disease, and more. Detecting health risks early allows for timely treatment and better outcomes.

How often should I get a health checkup done?

It depends on your age, lifestyle, and medical history. Generally, an annual checkup is recommended for adults. For people with existing conditions or risk factors, more frequent checkups may be necessary.
